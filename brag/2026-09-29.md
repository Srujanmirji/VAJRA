# Brag doc: VAJRA, last 30 days (2026-08-30 → 2026-09-29)

I built and deployed VAJRA, a nowcasting system for thunderstorms, hail and cloudbursts over India at 0–6 hour lead times, for Smart India Hackathon 2026 (PS 26084, sponsored by NCMRWF/MoES). It covers everything from reading raw sensor data to a live forecaster console. It takes in radar, satellite and lightning data, detects hazards using published physics methods, and produces probabilistic forecasts with honest confidence levels. It also produces CAP 1.2 alerts that a forecaster checks before anything goes out. It's live at [vajra-ivory.vercel.app](https://vajra-ivory.vercel.app/console).

## Forecasting pipeline

- **Data intake and quality control on a 1 km grid.** Adapters for IMD Doppler radar, INSAT-3DR/3DS satellite imagery and lightning feeds. Quality control covers speckle and ground-clutter removal and Hitschfeld-Bordan attenuation correction. The data is merged onto a shared 1 km grid, with a PySteps extrapolation forecast as the baseline. `a3c2e72` (73 files). `TODO: impact?`
- **Early storm detection and hazard checks.** Satellite cloud-top cooling flags new storms 15–25 minutes before radar sees them (per the README's design claim). Hazard checks cover hail (Waldvogel POH and MESH), downbursts (velocity dipoles), cloudbursts (the IMD ≥100 mm/h rule) and Schultz lightning jumps. Storm cells are tracked and each location gets a live arrival countdown. `ceb5188`
- **Forecast blending and verification.** Optical-flow extrapolation and a 16-member STEPS ensemble plus a 9-channel U-Net are blended with IMD-HRRR/NCUM-R model output, weighted by lead time. A verification engine produces confidence tables, so each forecast horizon shows how reliable it is. `185ef2b`

## Forecaster product

- **Real-time server.** FastAPI REST and WebSocket server running a live pipeline, with a replay controller for past events. It generates CAP 1.2 XML alerts with polygons, and SMS/IVR text in English, Hindi, Bengali and Kannada. `a71a0a8`
- **Next.js dashboard.** Console, overview, method and status pages, plus a demo script. `ac02590` (+5.8k lines, the largest single commit)
