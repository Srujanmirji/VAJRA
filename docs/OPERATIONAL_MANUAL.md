# VAJRA Operational Forecaster Manual & Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

**Document Code:** VAJRA-SOP-2026-V1  
**Target Audience:** Duty Forecasters, Regional Meteorological Centres (RMCs), Air Traffic Management Meteorologists, and Disaster Relief Coordinators  
**Authority:** NCMRWF / Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES)

---

## 1. Operational Philosophy: Decision Support, Not Autonomous Alarms

VAJRA is designed as an **expert decision-support system**. 
- **Rule 1:** VAJRA never broadcasts emergency sirens or public SMS alerts autonomously.
- **Rule 2:** Every alert generated by the system enters the `PENDING_IMD_APPROVAL` queue and must be verified and authorized by the Meteorologist-on-Duty.
- **Rule 3:** Numerical Weather Prediction (NWP) background fields are strictly read-only and never modified.

---

## 2. Shift Handover & Daily Routine Checklist

At the start of each 8-hour shift, the duty forecaster must complete the following routine:

1. **Check Pipeline Status (`/status`):**
   - Verify that the **Last Cycle** executed within the last 5 minutes.
   - Confirm that total execution duration is under **60,000 ms** (typically ~2.2 s).
   - Check all four **Multi-Sensor Health Chips**:
     - *Radar:* Active DWR (e.g. VECC Kolkata / Dehradun), latency $\le 3\text{ min}$, coverage $> 98\%$.
     - *INSAT:* MOSDAC 3DS TIR-1, latency $\le 15\text{ min}$.
     - *Lightning:* Ground stream active, latency $\le 20\text{ s}$.
     - *NWP:* Latest operational cycle (00Z/06Z/12Z/18Z) loaded.
2. **Review Active Weather Map (`/console`):**
   - Inspect the current $T+0\text{ min}$ radar reflectivity composite.
   - Toggle to **Convective Initiation (CI)** layer to spot any nascent thermal cooling zones ($\le -4\text{ K / 15 min}$) in cloud fields.
   - Scrub through the lead time slider from $+0\text{m}$ to $+60\text{m}$ to review predicted cell trajectories and uncertainty envelopes.

## 3. Interpreting the Forecaster Console (`/console`)

### 3.1 Active Storms & Sparklines
The left panel displays all tracked convective cells ($\ge 40\text{ dBZ}$):
- **Cell ID:** Unique identifier (e.g., `CELL_KOL_01`).
- **Severity Badge:** 
  - `MODERATE` ($40–49\text{ dBZ}$): Thunderstorm with moderate rain.
  - `SEVERE` ($50–55\text{ dBZ}$): Hail risk and strong convective gusts.
  - `EXTREME` ($>55\text{ dBZ}$): Severe hail supercell or cloudburst core.
- **Sparkline:** Past 20-minute peak reflectivity trend. Upward curve indicates rapid intensification.

### 3.2 Live Arrival Countdowns & Uncertainty Windows
The countdowns tab lists major airports, cities, and vulnerable basins:
- **Countdown Display:** Live `MM:SS` countdown until the storm reaches the location boundary.
- **Arrival Window:** 10th-to-90th percentile arrival window (e.g. `35–55 min`).
- **Probability:** Percentage of ensemble members penetrating the target location within 60 minutes.
- *Operational Action:* If an airport has probability $\ge 60\%$ with an impending window of $30–45\text{ min}$, notify Air Traffic Control (ATC) watch supervisors for runway management.

### 3.3 Physical Hazard Panels
- **Hail (POH & MESH):** If $\text{POH} \ge 70\%$ and $\text{MESH} \ge 25\text{ mm}$, hail damage to standing crops and tin-roof housing is imminent.
- **Downburst Wind:** If $|\Delta v| \ge 20\text{ m/s}$ ($72\text{ km/h}$), issue immediate crosswind and microburst shear guidance to airport tower.
- **Cloudburst:** If an IMD cloudburst cluster ($\ge 100\text{ mm/h}$ over $\ge 20\text{ km}^2$) is detected in mountain catchments, issue immediate flash flood alert to State Emergency Operations Centre (SEOC).

```
[Hazard Breached] ──> [CAP Draft Generated] ──> [Forecaster Reviews Map]
                                                        │
                      ┌─────────────────────────────────┴───────────────────┐
                      ▼                                                     ▼
           [Click "Approve & Sign-Off"]                         [Click "Reject Alert"]
                      │                                                     │
       [Status: APPROVED by Forecaster]                        [Record Reason: e.g. Clutter]
                      │                                                     │
[Broadcasts to NDMA SACHET, DAMINI, SMS/IVR]                     [Logged in Audit Trail]
```

### Step-by-Step Approval Protocol:
1. When a warning appears under the **CAP Alerts** tab with status `PENDING_IMD_APPROVAL`, click the card to inspect the alert details.
2. Review the polygon on the map: confirm that the warning area corresponds to genuine meteorological echoes and not anomalous propagation or ground clutter.
3. Check the four language previews (**English, Hindi, Bengali, Kannada**) in the SMS/IVR preview modal.
4. Click **"Approve & Sign-Off"**. Your official Forecaster ID and timestamp will be cryptographically attached to the CAP XML bulletin.
5. If the echo is judged non-threatening (e.g. decaying shower), click **"Reject"** and enter a brief rationale.

## 5. Contingency Procedures for Sensor Degradation

| Failure Mode | Visual Indicator in UI | Operational System Fallback |
|---|---|---|
| **Radar Feed Failure / Offline** | Radar chip turns RED (`OFFLINE`); radar layer clears | VAJRA automatically switches to **Satellite + Lightning** mode. Resolution drops to 4 km; areas are hatched with "Lower Confidence (Satellite Guidance)" warning. |
| **INSAT Satellite Gap** | Satellite chip turns AMBER (`DELAYED`) | System continues on Radar + Lightning. CI detection is paused until fresh satellite frame arrives. |
| **Lightning Stream Interrupted** | Lightning chip turns AMBER (`STALE`) | Radar advection and MESH hail calculations continue uninterrupted; lightning jump warnings are suspended. |
| **NWP Model Feed Missing** | NWP chip indicates `PREVIOUS_CYCLE_HELD` | Lead times 2–6 h use persistence extrapolation with clear confidence degradation warning in the confidence table. |
