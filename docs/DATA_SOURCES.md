# Data Sources, Sensor Specifications & Licensing

VAJRA operates on a multi-sensor data fusion philosophy: combining high-resolution active microwave Doppler radar scans with passive geostationary satellite thermal infrared and ground lightning electromagnetic strokes.

---

## 🛰️ Sensor Specifications & Ingest Specifications

| Dataset | Native Format | Spatial Resolution | Update Cadence | Key Physical Parameters | Role in VAJRA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **IMD Doppler Weather Radar** | ODIM HDF5 | 250 m radial $\times$ 1° azimuth | 5–10 min | Reflectivity ($Z$), Radial Velocity ($V$), Spectrum Width ($W$) | Core high-resolution convective core and wind shear tracking |
| **INSAT-3DR / 3DS Imager** | MOSDAC HDF5 | 4 km at nadir | 15 min | Thermal IR (10.8 µm, 12.0 µm), Water Vapor (6.8 µm) | Early convective initiation cooling, radar gap coverage |
| **Ground Lightning Network** | ASCII / GeoJSON | Point locations ($\approx 500\text{ m}$) | Real-time ($<20\text{ s}$) | Stroke coordinates, peak current (kA), polarity, CG/IC flag | Updraft surge proxy, Schultz $2\sigma$ lightning jump detector |
| **IMD-HRRR / NCUM-R** | NetCDF-4 | 1.5 km – 4 km | 6-hourly run | Precipitation rate, temperature aloft, convective CAPE | Read-only synoptic background for 2–6 h lead-time blend |
| **SEVIR Benchmark** | HDF5 | 1 km / 2 km | 5 min | Multi-channel storm sequences (radar, IR, GLM) | Pre-training weights for 9-channel `VajraNowcastUNet` |
| **pysteps-data** | NetCDF / HDF5 | 1 km | 5 min | Radar reflectivity sequences | Baseline benchmark for optical flow and STEPS verification |

## 🏷️ Provenance & Labeling Standard

To preserve total transparency for meteorologists and jury evaluation:
- **`LIVE`:** Real-time data ingested directly from operational feeds.
- **`REPLAY`:** Historical real storm event replayed sequentially with authentic time-stamps (e.g. Kolkata Nor'wester, SEVIR sequences).
- **`SIMULATED`:** Physics-based synthetic convective sequence generated by `backend/app/simulators/india_sim.py` for testing and demonstration prior to receiving proprietary IMD radar feeds.

Every data card, map overlay, and JSON payload in VAJRA bears an explicit `provenance` field.
